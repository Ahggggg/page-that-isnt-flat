No ink, or no ink yet?

Survey register

What we searched, and what each search rules out

In September we searched three scrolls then listed for the First Letters prize (PHerc. 1203, 0800 and 1447) and accepted no writing. Every search was first run on known writing, this page of PHerc. 139, to show it could see letters at all. One of those nulls has since been overtaken: see the last entry.

    Method and reproduction

    How it was done

    Input
    PHerc0139 w035: tifxyz mesh, 2.399 µm CT volume 20260102150214, the organizers’ 109-layer surface volume and their published prediction. Vesuvius Challenge open data, CC BY-NC 4.0.
    Model
    scrollprize/ink_canonical_2um @075855bc, hash-checked weights-only load. The band was run on an RTX 4090 whose convolutions most likely used TensorFloat-32, PyTorch’s default there: strict FP32 on CPU or another GPU gives map values within 0.009 and label AUCs within 0.0002 of these maps.
    Runner
    Our canonical runner, one of several public ones: 256 px tiles, stride 128, Hann blend, window centred on the surface. Reproduces the published map at r = over the band; all 8,332 input chunks byte-identical to the public files.
    Labels
    The organizers’ ink labels and supervision mask for w035 at 2.399 µm (published 21 September 2026), used only to score the runs and not shown. They cover the upper part of the band; about 30 mm² is scored (pixels at 9.6 µm, labelled ink against labelled papyrus). A 180° rotation of the labels scores , at chance. The other-pages test used the same kind of labels for w030, w039–w041 and w043–w045.
    Sheet depth
    Per output pixel, the depth of the brightest papyrus layer among the 109 (Gaussian prior about the surface, σ = 30 layers). CT only.
    Preregistration
    Window offsets, band, statistics and kill conditions fixed before any output. The tolerance prediction passed; the map-predicts-reading prediction did not (see “Why it matters”). The label scoring was preregistered too. Its first run stopped on a coverage gate set too high for the labelled area, and the rerun changed only that gate. Our own prediction that reading quality would fall about as fast as map agreement failed: it falls much more slowly.
    Limits
    One scroll. The map, the band and the window curve come from one segment; the tolerance was then checked on seven more labelled pages, first on 6 mm crops and then on four full labelled regions. Why w044 is steeper is undetermined: a tilted sheet, a depth-sensitive model and misplaced labels were tested and none is supported. The published map is itself a model output, so it favours the traced surface. The labels were probably among this model family’s training labels, so the AUC is not a held-out accuracy; differences below about 0.01 are not resolved on the w035 band, and below about 0.03–0.05 on the crops. The brightest-layer estimate can pick a neighbouring layer where sheets touch.
    Prior work
    Window centring: flummoxjr, kadenpool and AndreasHad04 (ScrollPrize/villa #1765, #1766, #1813). Sheet wander off the trace: the organizers’ Hecate model card and 2026 open-problems page. Transferred meshes and depth search: TAUIL Abd Elilah (#1912). The PHerc. 1447 location: the organizers’ team, as relayed by Bullo27’s first-letters survey.
    How the film was made

    What is measured, and what is animation

    Unrolling
    The 3D sheet and the flat page are both the organizers’ mesh. The motion between them is an animation, not a physical unrolling.
    Scroll cutaway
    The exterior and the cut face come from the scroll’s own CT. Lighting, materials, the lift-off and the glow are presentation.
    Reading window
    Every map in “The window” is a separate model run at that offset, shown run by run and never blended. (The standalone film crossfades between neighbouring runs and slides the curve’s marker between them.)
    Crumpled page
    Height is the CT-measured sheet offset, exaggerated ×6 and smoothed over about 70 µm, as stated on screen.
    Cross-sections
    Depth is stretched for visibility, growing on screen from true scale; the stretch control shows the factor.
    Never altered
    No letters or ink were drawn, painted or enhanced. Every number on screen comes from the analysis. Call-outs are placed by hand.
    Every scan and ink map here is computed from the data.
